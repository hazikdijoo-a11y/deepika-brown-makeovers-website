About

A steady hand on your biggest day

Deepika Brown Makeovers is a bridal and party makeup studio in Ernakulam, Kerala. Every girl deserves to feel like her best self on the days that matter, and that takes an artist who understands both the occasion and the skin underneath the look.

Clients come back for makeup that photographs beautifully and holds through hours of ceremony, dancing and heat, without cracking, fading or turning oily by evening.
